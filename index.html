Operating approach

How Ozofryn works.

Every requirement is reviewed on its commercial and operational merits. We confirm the product, specification, quantity, location, timing, delivery basis and payment structure before issuing a firm commercial position. Documentation and logistics are then coordinated through delivery and close-out.

01

Requirement

Product, grade, quantity, location, timing and buyer details are reviewed.

02

Commercial structure

Availability, pricing basis, delivery terms and payment structure are agreed.

03

Documentation & logistics

Contract, product documents, inspection and movement requirements are coordinated.

04

Delivery & close-out

Delivery records, quantity and quality documents, invoicing and any post-delivery matters are completed.

Responsibility

Controls that support physical trade.

Counterparty review, sanctions screening, product documentation and operational controls form part of our transaction process. Our requirements vary according to the product, market, delivery structure and counterparty.
