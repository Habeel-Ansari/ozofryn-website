Products

Core products.

Ozofryn's trading portfolio is centred on gasoil, marine gasoil, fuel oil and base oil. Specifications, origin, packaging, quantity and delivery terms vary by market and supply position. A product is offered only against the documents and parameters agreed for the transaction.

Gasoil

Supplied for transport, industrial, power and regional distribution requirements, depending on the grade and applicable market standard.

Commercial role Transport, industrial or regional supply depending on grade
Available grades Diesel 10 ppm (≤10 ppm sulphur), AGO, ULSD (≤15 ppm sulphur)
Delivery FOB or CIF; ULSD also on a DES basis
Documents Product specification and available quality or safety documents may be shared with qualified counterparties, subject to transaction stage and supplier permissions.

Marine Gas Oil

Supplied for vessel operations and marine fuel requirements at confirmed delivery locations.

Commercial role Marine distillate for vessel operations and bunker supply
Specification ≤0.1% sulphur, supplied for MARPOL-compliant vessel operation
Delivery Ex-Wharf or DES — see Bunkering for supply locations
Documents Product specification and available quality or safety documents may be shared with qualified counterparties, subject to transaction stage and supplier permissions.

Fuel Oil

Supplied for marine and industrial applications in grades suited to the stated use and delivery market.

Commercial role Marine and industrial residual fuels
Available grades VLSFO (≤0.5% sulphur, IMO 2020) and HSFO (≤3.5% sulphur, for scrubber-fitted vessels)
Delivery Ex-Wharf or DES
Documents Product specification and available quality or safety documents may be shared with qualified counterparties, subject to transaction stage and supplier permissions.

Base Oil

Supplied as feedstocks for lubricant blending and for approved industrial applications.

Commercial role Feedstock for lubricant blending and industrial applications
Available grades Confirmed against enquiry and supply position
Packaging Packaging and bulk delivery options confirmed against the enquiry
Documents Product specification and available quality or safety documents may be shared with qualified counterparties, subject to transaction stage and supplier permissions.
