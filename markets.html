Markets

Two markets. Defined operating roles.

Ozofryn's business is organised around the UAE and India. Each market has a distinct role within the group's trading and customer coverage.

United Arab Emirates

Regional & international trading base.

The UAE is Ozofryn's base for regional and international petroleum trading. The operation develops supply relationships, reviews bunker and bulk enquiries, structures commercial terms and coordinates cross-border transactions. Activity is focused on gasoil, marine gasoil, fuel oil and base oil.

India

Domestic coverage & customer coordination.

The India operation supports domestic market coverage, local customer development and transaction coordination within its approved legal and licensing scope. It works with the UAE team on relevant product flows while maintaining a separate operational and commercial role.

Entity structure

How the two operations relate.

The UAE operation manages regional and international trading activity. The India operation supports domestic market development and customer coordination. Each maintains its own legal and commercial responsibilities within its market, coordinating on shared product flows where relevant.

Ozofryn trades and supplies marine and industrial petroleum products across the UAE and India.
