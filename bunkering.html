Bunkering

Marine fuel supply for clearly defined port requirements.

Ozofryn supports bunker requirements for shipowners, managers, operators, charterers and trading counterparties. Each enquiry is assessed against the vessel schedule, port, product grade, quantity, delivery method and available supply position.

Products

Grades relevant to the ports we serve.

Marine Gas Oil

Marine distillate supplied against the specification agreed in the nomination and contract.

Specification ≤0.1% sulphur, MARPOL-compliant
Delivery Ex-Wharf or DES

Fuel Oil

Residual marine fuels supplied against confirmed viscosity, sulphur and quality requirements.

Grades VLSFO (≤0.5% sulphur, IMO 2020) and HSFO (≤3.5% sulphur, scrubber-fitted vessels)
Delivery Ex-Wharf or DES
Supply locations

Confirmed ports only.

Fujairah, UAE.

Product and delivery availability remains subject to the specific port, volume, timing and supplier position.

From enquiry to delivery

A defined review process.

We review the vessel details, requested grade, quantity, ETA and delivery location before confirming a position. Once commercial terms are agreed, the nomination, delivery arrangements and required product documents are coordinated with the parties involved. Delivery records are checked as part of transaction close-out.

Quality & quantity

Verified at each stage.

Product specification, sampling, measurement, survey and documentation requirements are agreed for each supply. Independent inspection may be arranged where required by the contract and accepted by the parties.
