Bulk Trading

Bulk petroleum supply for regional and cross-border requirements.

Ozofryn structures spot and term supply for distributors, industrial consumers, blenders and established trading counterparties. Our bulk portfolio covers gasoil, fuel oil and base oil, with marine gasoil included where it forms part of a cargo requirement.

Commercial scope

Structures supported where the desk can deliver.

Transactions are considered against product specification, volume, origin, loading or discharge location, delivery window, Incoterm and payment structure. FOB, CFR, CIF, DES and ex-tank terms are offered only where the relevant supply and logistics can support them.

Execution

Coordinated documentation and logistics.

Depending on the transaction, Ozofryn coordinates product documentation, contractual terms, inspection, vessel or local transport arrangements, terminal interfaces and discharge documentation. Responsibilities are defined in the contract and supporting transaction documents.

Delivery terms

Owned capability distinguished from third-party arrangements.

Delivery terms, typical customers, logistics coordination and documentation are agreed per transaction. Where a service is provided through a third party, this is stated clearly rather than presented as an Ozofryn capability.
