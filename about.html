About Ozofryn

A focused petroleum trading and supply business.

Ozofryn operates across the UAE and India, with activity centred on marine bunkering and bulk petroleum trading. We work with buyers, suppliers and service partners to structure physical supply around clear commercial terms, product requirements and delivery conditions.

Our business

Two divisions, one operating model.

The company focuses on four product groups: gasoil, marine gasoil, fuel oil and base oil. The UAE operation manages regional and international trading activity. The India operation supports domestic market development and local customer coordination.

How we work

Scope defined for every transaction.

Ozofryn's role depends on the transaction. It may include product sourcing, commercial negotiation, documentation, inspection coordination, shipping or local logistics, and delivery follow-up. The scope is set out clearly for each transaction. We do not present third-party assets or services as our own.
